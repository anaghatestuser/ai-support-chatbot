# Stride Shoes - Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Returns

We offer a **30-day return policy** on all orders.

**Conditions for return:**
- Items must be unworn and in original condition
- Items must be returned in their original box and packaging
- Final sale items are not eligible for return

**How to start a return:**
1. Email support@strideshoes.com with your order number and reason for return
2. We will send you a prepaid return shipping label within 1 business day
3. Drop off the package at any USPS location

**Refund timeline:** Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days of receiving the returned item. The refund will appear on your original payment method.

## Sizing

We recommend using our **size guide** available on each product page before ordering.

**Tips:**
- For running shoes, we recommend going **half a size up** from your regular shoe size, as running shoes are designed to have a little extra room for foot swell.
- For casual and lifestyle shoes, your standard size is usually correct.
- **Wide widths** are available on select styles. Look for the "W" (wide) or "XW" (extra wide) size options on product pages.

If you are between sizes or unsure, contact our support team for personalized recommendations.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a **shipping confirmation email** containing a tracking link and your tracking number.

**To track your order:**
1. Open your shipping confirmation email
2. Click the tracking link, or visit the carrier's website and enter your tracking number

**Where to find your order number:**
- In your order confirmation email (sent immediately after purchase)
- In your account dashboard under "Order History" if you created an account

If you did not receive a confirmation email, check your spam folder or contact us at support@strideshoes.com.
